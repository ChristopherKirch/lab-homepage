Mt. Baldy Hiking Trails

Mt. Baldy has hiking trails for experienced hikers

Mt. Baldy has four peaks.

  • Best times for hiking: June to October
  • Risks: exposed hiking and inclement weather
  • Free parking without permits

Mt. Baldy is a popular hiking destination.

Necessary hiking equipment includes water, food, and a map

Some hikers at Mt. Baldy have struggled with altitude sickness.
