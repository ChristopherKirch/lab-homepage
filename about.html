About

Mt. Baldy is viewable from the Robert Day Science Center at Claremont McKenna College

The Roberts Environmental Center has led conservation projects at Mt. Baldy.

  • Approximately a 30 minute drive from RDSC to Mt. Baldy
  • North on Columbia Avenue, turn onto Baseline Road, and follow Mount Baldy Road
  • Local dining options include the Mt. Baldy village

Mt. Baldy was featured on CMC's Rose Parade float in 2023.

Mt. Baldy serves as a natural escape for CMC students

Climbing Mt. Baldy is on the bucket list of many students
